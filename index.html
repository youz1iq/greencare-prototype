GreenCare

Green space care system: a watering schedule, confirmation that the work was done, and a record of plant condition. Gardeners see their tasks for the day in a mobile app, and the facilities department plans and checks the work.

  • Daily task list
  • GPS check-in and check-out
  • Plant condition record
  • Works offline

ICT midterm project · interactive prototype (demo login, no password)
